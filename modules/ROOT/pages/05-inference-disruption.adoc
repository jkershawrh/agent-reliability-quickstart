= 5. Build: Degrade safely during inference disruption

Trigger the controlled inference timeout:

[source,bash]
----
curl -fsS "$ROUTE/api/v1/advise" -H 'content-type: application/json' \
  -d '{"query":"Diagnose NOC-1042","failure":"inference_timeout"}' | jq
----

The outcome is `degraded`, model status is `unavailable`, and the response
directs the operator to the approved manual runbook. It must not claim that a
diagnosis or remediation occurred. Bounded retries prevent an outage from
turning into an unbounded queue of model calls.
